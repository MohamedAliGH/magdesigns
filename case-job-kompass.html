A calmer way through the job hunt

Ten of ten surveyed job seekers were tracking applications with no dedicated tool, and zero of ten would trust a bare match score. The response replaces the score with a reasoned, plain-language read, and treats applying, watching, deciding later, and declining as four equally valid outcomes.
