01

Removing friction from a high-stakes credit funnel

Redesigning a financial application flow to surface every step without hiding it behind a swipe.

A. Challenge

A carousel hid steps users had already committed to — discoverability relied on a swipe many never made.

B. Approach

Explored three directions, shipped vertical disclosure cards on an existing design-system component — every step visible, accessible.

C. Outcome

A fully visible, accessible application; post-launch result being instrumented.

  • Conversion
  • Accessibility

Smart Repayment Guard

A context-aware intervention that warns without blocking — preserving autonomy while surfacing real risk.

A. Challenge

Good at “how much can I take?”, silent on “should I?” — risk hides in repeat withdrawals under stress.

B. Approach

A context-aware bottom sheet that warns without blocking — future-cost snapshot, soft alternatives, explicit choice.

C. Outcome

Concept / stakeholder proposal with a defined test plan (projected, not results).

03

From five products to one operational hub

Unifying a fragmented enterprise ecosystem — one SSO, one IA, a shared design system.

A. Challenge

Six products behaved like five startups — multiple logins, manually stitched KPIs, no shared IA.

B. Approach

Reframed fragmentation as infrastructure — one SSO, one IA, a workspace home; founded the design system.

C. Outcome

One operational hub, and a design system that outlived the portal.

04

Predictive maintenance, legible at a glance

Turning dense sensor data into a three-level view where failing machines stand out immediately.

A. Challenge

All the sensor data, almost none of the legibility — failing machines didn’t stand out.

B. Approach

Three legible levels (Fleet / Asset / Signal), each answering one question; severity over completeness.

C. Outcome

Fleet triage moved from an undifferentiated table to a severity-sorted view.
